# SCMT 205 Study Guide

A free, student-made study guide for **SCMT 205 (CC02) Supply Chain Technology Management**, Fall 2026, MacEwan University.

**Open it:** https://kingstonm29.github.io/SCMT-205-/ (once GitHub Pages is on, see below)

## What's inside

- **Dashboard**: countdown to the next quiz or exam, a clickable term calendar (Sept–Dec) with every class, quiz, deadline and closure, upcoming dates, grade weights, your progress by topic, and how the instructor tends to write quiz questions.
- **Schedule**: the full term from the course outline, with "add to Google Calendar" buttons.
- **Notes**: organized by week (Weeks 1–5, 7 and 8 so far, plus the Pagano & Liotine Ch. 2 reading), each ending with what's most likely to be tested.
- **Live diagrams**: bullwhip simulator, SCOR DS loop, point-to-point vs hub integration, perfect order index, OEE, ATP vs CTP, weighted decision matrix, supplier TCO, three-way match, cash-to-cash, payback/ROI, digital maturity roadmap, automation spectrum, WMS/WES/WCS stack, digital model vs shadow vs twin.
- **Flashcards**: 240+ cards, filterable by week or quiz, with "still learning" tracking.
- **Practice**: a quiz for each week plus Quiz 1, 2 and 3 review sets that run in week-by-week sections. True/false, multiple choice and short essays in the style of the instructor's two mock quizzes (Quiz 1: 12 + 8; Quiz 2: 10 + 8), with explanations. The Quiz 2 set includes Week 7 because the Quiz 2 mock quiz tests it.
- **Grades**: calculator using the course weights and MacEwan's letter scale.

Everything runs in one file (`index.html`). Your scores, flashcard progress and marks are saved only in your own browser.

## Turning on the public link (repo owner)

Settings → Pages → Build and deployment → Source: **Deploy from a branch** → pick the branch with `index.html` and `/ (root)` → Save. The site appears at `https://kingstonm29.github.io/SCMT-205-/` within a minute or two.
